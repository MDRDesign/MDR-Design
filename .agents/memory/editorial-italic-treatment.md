---
name: Editorial italic treatment
description: Scope and visibility expectations for italic color accents and highlight sweeps.
---

Italic color and highlight treatments must include the Approach wording (“say out loud” and “Listen”) and every italic on red backgrounds, not just the opening and dark-section headlines.

**Why:** The owner specifically called out those omissions and reported that the previous sweeping animation was not visibly working.

**How to apply:** Include light-background italics when refining this treatment, using readable shades. Verify visible sweep movement at desktop and mobile sizes, not merely that an animation name exists. Keep the closing color-changing-background italic matched to the opening reddish accent, and honor reduced-motion preferences.

Use dark lettering for red-band display italics, with a light sweep underneath rather than a light highlight passing through the text.

**Why:** The owner approved the refined treatment but requested a darker alternative to gold on red. A transition from dark to light lettering would cross a low-contrast range against crimson.

**How to apply:** Keep dark-on-red italics at accessible large-display sizes; do not extend this treatment to small text without rechecking contrast. Confirm that the sweep is actually displayed, since a computed animation can exist on a hidden pseudo-element.
