name: Service positioning
description: Business app development is a core MDR Design service, not a secondary web-design feature.
---

Business app development must be presented as a major service alongside website design and branding, without making the portfolio less streamlined.

**Why:** The owner said they are clearly building apps for businesses, that it is “a big part of the services I offer,” and asked to include it without adding clutter.

**How to apply:** Give app development clear visibility in studio positioning and service inquiries. Keep the existing compact editorial approach; do not bury apps in website-design wording or add unnecessary sections to make the point. Describe web apps honestly rather than implying native mobile development.

Captain’s Galley and Z-Delivery are owner-approved examples for explaining business-app solutions.

**Why:** The owner explicitly authorized either project as an example of developing an app to solve a business problem or need.

**How to apply:** Use their verified functionality to explain the need and solution. Approval to feature them does not establish client testimonials, measured outcomes, or permission to expose protected app content.

Use the owner-provided Z-Delivery mobile captures as app evidence rather than the public sign-in page. The customer details in those original screenshots are fictional and may remain visible, including in quoting and map screens.

**Why:** The owner confirmed, “The customer details showing are already fake for this use,” and explicitly authorized revealing the details in the original quote and map photos.

**How to apply:** Preserve these original screenshots without redaction and describe their customer data as fictional. Do not treat captured quote amounts or status counts as measured project results, or extend this permission to other data.
