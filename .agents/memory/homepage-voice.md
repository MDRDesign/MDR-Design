---
name: Homepage voice
description: How the hero should address prospective business owners.
---

Keep the homepage headline positive, collaborative and forward-thinking. A confident call-out is welcome, but it must not insult business owners or imply their business has not been serious until now.

**Why:** The owner disliked the implication of “Your business, finally looking like it means it” while still appreciating its confident directness.

**How to apply:** Frame future homepage headline revisions around shared ambition and what the owner and studio can build together, rather than criticizing the business's current standing.
